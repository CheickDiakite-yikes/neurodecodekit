# Code Of Conduct

## Our Commitment

NeuroDecodeKit is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically honest
community. Participation should be safe for people across identities, careers,
disciplines, abilities, experience levels, and access to research hardware.

We especially want contributors without institutional compute, large datasets,
or expensive MEG systems to be able to participate meaningfully.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas, code, protocols, and evidence without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and explain field-specific language when practical.
- Credit prior work and contributors accurately.
- Respect consent, privacy, dataset licenses, and participant dignity.
- State uncertainty and negative results without ridicule or pressure to hype.
- Stop and ask before sharing material that may be sensitive or proprietary.
- Accept maintainer decisions that a risky experiment must be preregistered,
  narrowed, or parked.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, or stalking.
- Publishing another person's private information or recordings.
- Pressuring someone to disclose neural data, medical history, demographics,
  credentials, or private communications.
- Misrepresenting research as clinical, diagnostic, therapeutic, consumer-ready,
  or capable of reading arbitrary thoughts.
- Knowingly bypassing holdout, licensing, consent, security, or resource rules.
- Plagiarism, fabricated results, hidden target leakage, or deceptive reporting.
- Disruptive behavior that makes technical collaboration unsafe or impossible.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, reviews,
community spaces, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting

Do not report sensitive conduct details in a public issue. Use the repository's
private security-advisory reporting route when it is available, with
`Code of conduct report` in the title, or contact the lead maintainer through
the GitHub profile at https://github.com/CheickDiakite-yikes and request a
private reporting channel without including the incident details publicly.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. People named in a report
will not be assigned to investigate that report. Retaliation against a reporter
or witness is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant depending on the
severity, impact, context, and pattern of behavior. A maintainer with a conflict
of interest must recuse from the decision.

The goal is a community where careful engineering and scientific humility can
coexist with curiosity and ambition.
