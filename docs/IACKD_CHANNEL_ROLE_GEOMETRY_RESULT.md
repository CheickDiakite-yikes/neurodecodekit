reader assumed:

1. all 128 channel tables reproduce the H1 split: 96 have 29 rows and 32 have
   31 rows;
2. all sidecars and present channel sampling declarations agree at 1024 Hz;
3. removing only optional M1/M2 leaves one ordered core schema;
4. every run declares the same 26 predictive channels with source type `EEG`;
5. all 128 sidecars declare average reference;
6. all 30 geometry groups have finite C3, C4, Cz, O1, Oz, and O2; and
7. all 26 predictive EEG channels have finite geometry in every group.

The 30 geometry groups form two aggregate signatures: 22 groups have 29
electrode rows with 26 finite coordinates, while eight have 31 rows with 28
finite coordinates. The difference tracks optional M1/M2 availability; it
does not change the 26-channel predictive set.

## Why `IACKDR-R1` Applied

The source declarations contradicted two assumptions in the frozen audit, not
each other:

1. `HEOG` and `VEOG` are consistently typed `MISC`, while the frozen
   compatibility rule allowed only their special labels or `EOG`. Therefore
   `required_control_BIDS_roles_valid` is false.
2. The channel tables consistently type `HEOG`, `VEOG`, and `Trigger` as three
   `MISC` channels, and the sidecars consistently report `MiscChannelCount=3`,
   `EOGChannelCount=0`, and `TriggerChannelCount=0`. The frozen reconciler
   reclassified the named trigger into a separate trigger bucket, producing a
   two-MISC/one-trigger expectation. Therefore
   `present_sidecar_type_counts_reconcile` is false.

Trigger presence and sampling were valid. H1 row counts and allowlisted names
also reconciled. The failure is therefore a role-taxonomy mismatch in our
prospective contract, not evidence that source files are malformed.
