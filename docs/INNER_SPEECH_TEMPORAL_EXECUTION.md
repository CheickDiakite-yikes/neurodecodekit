# Fresh-session temporal test: approved execution boundary

October 2, 2026. The maintainer approved ten session-2 recordings (6.40 GiB,
60 minutes maximum), then one frozen 20-minute development experiment and one
score. This activates the earlier [source proposal](INNER_SPEECH_TEMPORAL_NEXT_TEST.md).
The exact [machine plan](../registries/inner_speech_temporal_plan.v0.json) is fixed
before new source acquisition or physiological access.

The scientific question is whether **ordered signed EEG waveform information**
adds imagined-word information beyond peripheral/timing controls, including
their temporal information, and beyond spectral EEG on identical trials.
We reuse dual ridge and nested calibration; there is no architecture, band,
regularization, feature-count or epoch search.

All ten people, all three conditions and thirteen arms remain. The primary
requires at least 0.02 nats mean joint gain over P and the same nine of ten
people strictly beating all six comparators, including the same-trial spectral
joint model. A separate EEG-only cue sensitivity gate requires nine of ten
beating its own shuffle, uniform and training prior, plus 0.02 nats mean gain
over uniform. Both gates are required even to propose untouched confirmation;
secondary results cannot rescue the primary.

Eligibility is prospective: 200 nominal slots per person, at most two uniquely
incomplete core slots per person (twenty total), no inferred label or timestamp,
and no person or condition dropped. Both independent event decoders must agree
exactly and all thirty split preflights must pass before physiology. The
author's participant-10 short-pulse rule applies to session 1 only; it is not
applied to session 2. Nor are session-1 baseline or condition exceptions reused.

Acquisition uses the pinned object versions and retains all original channels
outside OneDrive. All ten metadata-only object-header checks passed before
download. Generated tests validate decoding, fixed windows and dimensions,
matched controls, blocked/embargoed splits and one-shot publication. No test
fixture is a scientific result.

The complete acquisition receipt must be committed before prediction. All
prediction hashes must then be committed, pushed and remotely verified before
one score. The 20-minute limit includes this freeze and score. Any source,
decoder, model, process or resource failure ends the attempt; no automatic
retry, partial scoring or outcome-guided correction is authorized.

Session 1 remains consumed and unchanged; session 3 remains unopened and
unacquired. This is same-day within-person development, not new-day or
unseen-person confirmation. Neither a cue response nor a successful four-word
result establishes cortical origin, cue-independent thoughts, arbitrary text,
prospective live use or clinical utility.
