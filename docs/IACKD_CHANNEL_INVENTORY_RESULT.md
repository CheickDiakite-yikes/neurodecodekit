## What The Failure Actually Was

The consumed IACKD-1 reader required exactly 36 declared channels and required
M1, M2, HEOG, and VEOG to be present. The audit now establishes three facts:

1. neither observed declaration count is 36;
2. the first deterministic header belongs to the 29-channel group and lacks M1
   and M2, so both its exact-count and canonical-name gates fail; and
3. the dataset is heterogeneous across runs, so one exact global channel list
   cannot describe all 128 declarations.

The frozen `IACKDH-R5` route therefore applies. This explains the old
engineering stop without reopening or rescuing IACKD-1.

The declared totals are not labeled channel types. The actual EEG-channel
count, geometry, reference, bad-channel state, and signal quality remain
unavailable and must not be inferred from 29 or 31.
