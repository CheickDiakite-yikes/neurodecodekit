## Decision In One Page

NeuroDecodeKit will optimize for belief-changing evidence rather than proof
machinery. The flagship question remains whether central EEG adds predictive
information beyond recorded peripheral physiology, posterior EEG, cue and
timing structure, and matched derangement in completely unseen people. No

### Execution compression rule

Every substantive work block must target that named hypothesis with real neural
data and end in measured scientific evidence or an explicit measured blocker.
Supporting engineering is allowed only as the minimal final dependency of that
exact experiment and must lead directly to execution, not another nested gate.
The default route is real data -> target firewall -> compact model -> frozen
predictions -> one score -> independent replication. Held-out targets may not
drive tuning, and a positive result may not be manufactured. Reports must state
scientific evidence and supporting engineering separately. Existing Tier C
authority boundaries remain unchanged.

The governing architecture is a 3D attribution cube nested inside a 5D
scientific evidence map. Spatial, temporal, and physiological dimensions ask
what produced the signal. Task autonomy and population generalization ask what
the result means and how far it travels. A separate sixth translation
dimension orders offline evaluation, causal replay, shadow streaming, and
prospective live operation. Translation cannot rescue failed attribution,
identifiability, or generalization. Live motor success cannot validate
language decoding; communication remains separately preregistered with an
independently scored LLM-only baseline.

## C. Historical Dreyer Scientific Question

This section preserves the exact scientific design that motivated the current
fresh-source criteria. Its Dreyer lane is consumed and is not the active
execution plan.

In completely unseen participants performing visually cued left-versus-right
hand motor imagery, do predeclared central EEG features add held-out predictive
information beyond recorded EOG, wrist EMG, posterior EEG, trial timing, and a
matched deranged-EEG control?

This matters because it distinguishes a genuine incremental brain-sensor
contribution from the cheaper explanations that defeated the prior BNCI claim.
The [Dreyer primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02445-z)
reports 60 Dataset A participants, 27 EEG channels, three EOG electrodes, two
wrist-EMG electrodes, and 512 Hz sampling. The exact selected R1/R2 surface has
4,800 trials and a manageable 1.6575 GiB payload.

The strongest competing explanations are lateralized visual cue/feedback,
eye movement, covert or overt wrist muscle activity, trial timing, posterior
visual activity, cross-person sensor misalignment, and probability
miscalibration.

## E. Historical Dreyer Decisive Experiment

### Hypotheses

- `H0`: nuisance-residualized central EEG contributes no reliable held-out
  information beyond nuisance signals and matched derangement.
- `H1`: central EEG contributes at least `0.020` nats/trial against both
  nuisance-only and nuisance-plus-deranged-residual comparators, with
  participant consistency and calibrated above-baseline performance.
- `A_visual`: posterior/cue information explains the apparent effect.
- `A_peripheral`: EOG or wrist EMG explains the apparent effect.
- `A_timing`: run/trial structure explains the apparent effect.
- `A_alignment`: within-person information exists but fixed cross-person
  representation destroys it.
- `A_calibration`: discrimination exists but probability reliability fails.

### Frozen Test

- Representation: causal one-second 8-12, 12-20, and 20-30 Hz relative-power
  features; nine central homologous pairs; source-only nuisance residuals.
- Estimator: one source-standardized L2 logistic family with source-only
  temperature calibration.
- Generalization: zero-calibration held-out participant.
- Primary endpoint: participant-macro binary log loss.
- Co-primary effects: `LL(N)-LL(N+R)` and
  `LL(N+D(R))-LL(N+R)`, each at least `0.020`.
- Consistency: each effect positive in at least 39/60 participants with exact
  one-sided sign-test `p <= 0.025`.
- Mandatory checks: posterior removal, pre-cue and cue windows, equal prior,
  timing, EOG, EMG, posterior EEG, label rotation, balanced accuracy, log loss,
  calibration error, completeness, and zero post-target updates.

### Outcome Map

| Pattern | Belief update |
|---|---|
| `N+R` beats `N` and `N+D(R)` with consistency, while cue/pre controls fail | Supports incremental late central-EEG information in unseen participants |
| `N+R` beats `N` but not `N+D(R)` | Association exists, but matched EEG identity is not established |
| Posterior or cue conditions match the primary | Visual/task explanation remains viable; no central specificity |
| EOG/EMG dominate and residual EEG adds nothing | Peripheral protocol information explains performance |
| Accuracy improves but log loss/calibration fail | Discrimination without reliable probabilities; claim gate fails |
| Source/within-person checks work but LOSO fails | Prioritize alignment or calibration-law discovery |
| Positive controls fail | Pipeline or measurement sensitivity failure; do not infer signal absence |
| All primary comparisons are narrow around zero with positive controls intact | Evidence against a practically meaningful effect under this measurement/model class |

### Claim Coordinates At Maximum Pass

- Generalization: unseen participant, same dataset and task.
- Attribution: predictive increment beyond recorded nuisance sensors and
  spatial/temporal controls; not exclusive cortical causation.
- Temporal regime: sample-causal event-locked offline decision at eight seconds.
- Output: binary instructed protocol condition.
- Calibration: zero held-out-person calibration.
- Setting: curated laboratory.

## F. Invention Opportunities

These are discovery candidates, not changes to the frozen Dreyer confirmation.
No novelty claim is made before a dedicated literature and implementation
collision check.

### Candidate 1: Geometry-Normalized Residual Dynamics

- **Observed failure:** BNCI posterior EEG beat the selected representation,
  and participant consistency was weak.
- **Mechanism:** fixed sensor pairs may not align homologous sources across
  anatomy and cap placement.
- **Intervention:** align source-participant covariance or geometry before
  computing nuisance-orthogonal lateralized dynamics.
- **Expected signature:** cross-person gain rises while posterior, timing, and
  deranged controls do not.
- **Falsifier:** alignment raises every condition equally or only helps
  posterior/control channels.
- **Simplest comparator:** fixed homologous-pair residual features.
- **Closest analogues:** He and Wu's
  [Euclidean Alignment](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2019.2913914) and
  [tangent-space alignment](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2022.1049985).
- **True novelty:** currently unverified; any value would need to come from
  combining geometry with explicit nuisance-orthogonal attribution, not from
  alignment alone.
- **Minimum test:** source-only grouped cross-validation on an authorized
  development cohort; one fixed aligned arm versus one unaligned arm.
- **Failure value:** distinguishes misalignment from absent incremental signal.

### Candidate 2: Reliability-Gated Neural Increment

- **Observed failure:** selected EEG ranked classes above chance but had log
  loss worse than the equal prior.
- **Mechanism:** cross-person residual EEG is heteroscedastic; a fixed fusion
  overweights unreliable participant/epoch evidence.
- **Intervention:** estimate source-only residual reliability and shrink only
  the neural logit increment toward zero when uncertainty is high.
- **Expected signature:** log loss and calibration improve without increasing
  deranged-EEG or posterior-control gains.
- **Falsifier:** gains vanish under matched derangement or reduce only accuracy.
- **Simplest comparator:** existing source-only scalar temperature.
- **Closest analogue:** probability calibration and mixture-of-experts
  reliability weighting; no new scientific principle is claimed.
- **True novelty:** unverified integration at the neural-increment level.
- **Minimum test:** generated heteroscedastic fixture followed by one
  development-only grouped comparison.
- **Failure value:** tells us whether calibration, rather than representation,
  explains the prior gate failure.

### Candidate 3: Calibration Law Instead Of Zero-Shot Dogma

- **Observed failure:** aggregate signal can coexist with weak unseen-person
  consistency.
- **Mechanism:** a small participant-specific alignment sample may remove a
  large domain shift even when zero-shot decoding is brittle.
- **Intervention:** measure fixed 0, 1, 5, 10, and 20 labeled-trial calibration
  curves, with all global choices learned from source participants.
- **Expected signature:** a steep early gain with stable nuisance attribution
  identifies a favorable practical calibration law.
- **Falsifier:** calibration gains are no larger than nuisance-only gains or
  require impractical trial counts.
- **Simplest comparator:** zero-shot model and participant-only linear baseline.
- **Closest analogue:** weighted adaptation and broad BCI transfer-learning
  work; calibration reduction is established prior art.
- **True novelty:** none claimed. The scientific contribution would be an
  honest attribution-aware scaling law on the selected task.
- **Minimum test:** one authorized development cohort with nested participant
  splits; never the frozen Dreyer R1/R2 confirmation.
- **Failure value:** separates an invariance limit from a general signal limit.

### Unconventional Measurement Option

If cue/posterior confounding persists, change the task rather than the model:
orthogonalize visual cue side from instructed hand, remove lateralized visual
feedback, require central fixation, and record synchronized EOG and bilateral
EMG. This challenges the premise that legacy cued datasets can identify the
desired mechanism. It requires a future consented acquisition design and human
approval; it is not an immediate operation.
