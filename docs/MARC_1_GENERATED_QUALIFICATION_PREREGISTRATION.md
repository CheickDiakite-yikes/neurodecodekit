# MARC-1 Generated Qualification Preregistration

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: **generated-fixture-only contract frozen; implementation not started;
all public metadata, archive, payload, neural, target, model, and score access
unauthorized**

Contract: `registries/marc1_generated_qualification_contract.v0.json`

## Purpose

Qualify the two difficult mechanics MARC-1 needs before any real-data packet:

1. inspect a monolithic ZIP through bounded random-access reads without opening
   any member content or downloading the archive; and
2. validate modality roles, clocks, causal windows, split isolation, target
   withholding, and comparator availability without running a model.

This is software qualification on generated bytes. A success route has no
scientific meaning and cannot authorize a public request.

## Generated ZIP Fixture

Build one deterministic ZIP with Python's standard-library `zipfile` module.
The fixture contains exactly 14 safe regular-file members:

- four root metadata members; and
- five BIDS-like members for each of two generated participants.

Each participant has one `.eeg`, `.vhdr`, `.vmrk`, `_events.tsv`, and
`_channels.tsv` member. The `.eeg` writer must use `force_zip64=True` so the
qualification traverses a ZIP64 member record without allocating a large
payload. The complete generated archive must stay below 8 MiB.

The parser must use `zipfile.ZipFile` over an instrumented read-only seekable
range adapter. It may not hand-parse filenames or infer offsets with string
operations. The adapter receives only generated bytes and records every seek
and read.

Qualification requirements:

- exactly one archive inventory traversal;
- classic EOCD and ZIP64 member support through `zipfile`;
- no call to `ZipFile.open`, `ZipFile.read`, or member extraction;
- no read intersecting a member's compressed payload interval;
- at most 8 MiB total range bytes returned;
- at most 256 range reads;
- exact archive size and deterministic inventory replay;
- safe normalized POSIX-relative paths with no duplicates;
- regular files only, with no symlink, encryption, or unsupported compression;
- bounded member count, compressed size, uncompressed size, and ratio; and
- a private generated manifest separated from an aggregate public report.

The inventory records member name, CRC-32, compression method, flag bits,
compressed and uncompressed sizes, and local-header offset. The public report
may expose only aggregate counts, bytes, method counts, hashes, refusals, and
measurements. It must not expose a member name or offset.

## Generated Multimodal Plan

Build one generated plan containing two source profiles:

- a Freewill-like profile at 250 Hz with EEG, EOG, acceleration, and audio
  trigger roles; and
- a Wrist-like profile at 512 Hz with EEG, EMG, encoder, and task-trigger
  roles.

Every channel record must keep three facts separate:

```text
source type -> functional role -> model inclusion
```

Only source-declared EEG may enter a candidate or EEG-proxy model. EOG, EMG,
acceleration, encoder, audio, and trigger channels are controls or alignment
streams and are always nonpredictive for the EEG candidate.

Each channel record binds source, subject, session, run, name, source type,
functional role, model inclusion, sampling rate, units, sample count, source
start time, clock ID, synchronization state, and an explicit geometry state.
Unavailable geometry is a value, not an omitted field.

## Causal Window Contract

The generated candidate window is fixed to `[-1.5, -0.2)` seconds relative to
measured movement onset. The right endpoint is exclusive. The plan must state:

- causal preprocessing `true`;
- future context `0` samples;
- train-only normalization;
- no sample at or after the `0.2`-second onset guard;
- no centered, zero-phase, reflected-future, or bidirectional operation; and
- one CPU thread, one worker, and one numerical job.

This window is an interface fixture, not a frozen future scientific window.
The real window remains a later preregistration decision after metadata
qualification.

## Split And Target Firewall

Generated rows have exactly three physical roles:

1. `fit_rows` may contain generated fit labels;
2. `target_blind_prediction_rows` contain signal and identity only; and
3. `isolated_scorer_rows` contain the matching generated held-out targets.

Subject, session, run, trial, and window identities must be disjoint across
fit and held-out prediction groups. Prediction and scorer identities must
match exactly, while target fields must be absent from prediction rows. No
window-random split or cross-boundary overlap is permitted.

The plan must enumerate all twelve MARC-1 comparator roles and mark each as
`available` or `unavailable` by source. A comparator required by a source's
recorded modalities cannot be unavailable. Missing required availability makes
the future top route ineligible.

## Router

1. `MARC1G-F00`: contract, artifact, or green-proof mismatch.
2. `MARC1G-F01`: EOCD, ZIP64, range, archive, or resource failure.
3. `MARC1G-F02`: member path, duplicate, flag, method, ratio, or type failure.
4. `MARC1G-F03`: modality role, geometry, clock, or synchronization failure.
5. `MARC1G-F04`: causal preprocessing, future context, or onset-guard failure.
6. `MARC1G-F05`: split, target firewall, or comparator failure.
7. `MARC1G-F06`: output destination, privacy, overwrite, runtime, RSS, or cap
   failure.
8. `MARC1G-F07`: deterministic replay failure.
9. `MARC1G-R1`: all generated archive and multimodal gates pass.

generated private manifest. Inspection accepts only the aggregate report.

## Acceptance Gates

- both source-role profiles validate;
- the causal window and target firewall validate;
- all twelve comparator roles are represented;
- all 24 mutations refuse in the intended class;
- two independent generated replays have byte-identical canonical inventories
  and plans;
- runtime, RSS, output, and one-thread caps pass; and
- every public, real-data, signal, target, model, score, and claim counter is
  zero.
