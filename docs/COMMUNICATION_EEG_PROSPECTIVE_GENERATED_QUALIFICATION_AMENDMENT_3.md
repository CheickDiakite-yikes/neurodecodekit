# COMM-P0-G Amendment 3: Nonredundant Full-Scale Proof

Date: 2026-08-28
Amendment: `COMM-P0-G-A3`
Parent: `COMM-P0-G-v0`
Status: prospective generated-only proof-architecture correction; no operation authorized

## Why this amendment exists

The consumed nonofficial `COMM-P0-G-FS2-R0` resource rehearsal completed one
full fictional replay child but could not complete a second equivalent full
child inside the frozen 180-second total cap. It parked at
180.05074683297426 seconds. Peak RSS, storage, process monitoring, cleanup, and
all zero-operation gates passed.

The failure localizes the bottleneck to duplicated full numerical execution,
not memory or storage. Each full replay executes 42 isolated participant folds,
630 fixed-family classifier fits, 630 source-only temperature fits, 714
inference runs, 1,428 prediction sets, and 91,392 streamed prediction rows.
Repeating that complete schedule verifies software replay but adds no scientific
observation: every row, target, participant, signal, and outcome is fictional.

This amendment removes only redundant full-scale model fitting. It does not
change the scientific protocol or lower any future real-evidence gate.

## Replacement proof architecture

The two byte-equivalent full numerical replays are replaced by three
complementary proof surfaces:

1. **One complete full-scale producer child.** It executes the exact frozen
   42-participant, 17-condition schedule, creates the complete streamed
   prediction inventory, seals the prediction freeze before target delivery,
   runs both cohort scores and all seven shortcut fixtures, and preserves every
   missing/invalid-prediction penalty.
2. **One independent model-free verifier/scorer child.** It receives no model,
   fit, transform, threshold-selection, language-model, or update capability.
   Through no-follow descriptors it independently verifies contract and code
   identities, stream ordering and cardinality, file identity, freeze HMAC,
   delivery nonce, probability validity, missing/duplicate handling, and the
   aggregate score by a separate delivery of the same generated cohort targets
   after the freeze. Its aggregate score must match the producer score exactly.
3. **Reduced isolated determinism qualification.** Before activation, two fresh
   generated model children with at most three fictional participants per
   cohort must produce byte-equivalent canonical surfaces, and all 70 refusal
   families must be exercised once in each reduced replay. This preserves
   independent process and deterministic-mechanics proof without duplicating
   the full numerical workload.

The full producer must still exercise all 70 exact refusal families once after
its prediction freeze. The independent verifier must reject every scorer,
freeze, inventory, descriptor, and capability mutation assigned to it. No
malformed acceptance, wrong wrapper, state mutation after refusal, target leak,
or post-target model update is permitted.

## Fields unchanged

The following remain exactly frozen:

- all 10,752 structural rows, 73 sensor roles, two endpoints, 17 conditions,
  seven shortcut fixtures, participant-first statistics, and live accounting;
- the fixed source-scaled forward-bandpower multinomial L2 logistic family;
- source-only residualization, calibration, priors, thresholds, derangement,
  and language-only controls;
- target precommitment, held-out-person firewall, freeze-before-delivery,
  score-only capability, no replacement, and missing-prediction penalties;
- one CPU thread, one worker, one numerical job, 180 seconds, 512 MiB
  process-tree RSS, 513 MiB aggregate incremental disk, zero network, and zero
  retained generated payload bytes;
- all real/private, human, device, stream, release, and scientific authority
  remains false.

## Ordered barriers

1. This amendment and its test must be committed, pushed, pass both required CI
   jobs, and be present on GitHub `main`.
2. A separately named generated-only FS3 rehearsal contract must bind this
   exact amendment and the consumed FS2 result.
3. FS3 implementation may use only reduced or mocked fictional fixtures until
   its exact implementation and proof are remotely green on `main`.
4. One nonofficial FS3 rehearsal may then test the full producer plus
   independent verifier under the unchanged 180-second envelope.
5. Only a consumed FS3 pass may support a later separate official activation.

No barrier authorizes an official invocation, real/private access, a human or
device operation, network access, release, or scientific claim.
