# Next thought-to-text step: fresh imagined-word evidence

September 22, 2026. Source decision, not a new decoding result or data-access authorization.

The current five-word recordings cannot establish cue-independent thoughts or
new-day robustness. One fixed nested temperature test addresses the specific
accuracy/probability-quality mismatch; it must not become another model search.
The next larger scientific step is a fresh imagined-speech cohort with strong
peripheral comparisons. No language model is needed to answer that question.

## Reuse the existing Thinking Out Loud candidate

OpenNeuro **ds003626 v2.1.2** is the most practical reviewed candidate, already
selected in the August communication research lane. The descriptor reports
ten people, four Spanish directional words, imagined speech without mouth or
tongue movement, pronounced speech and visualization conditions, 128 EEG,
four EOG and two oral EMG electrodes. Its three consecutive sessions per person
were recorded on **one day**, not different days. Directional cues identify the
word even though they disappear before the action window.

Sources: [dataset paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01147-2),
[versioned CC0 description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds003626/2.1.2/dataset_description.json).

This supports a candidate **new-cohort, same-day attribution study**, not a
new-day replication or cue-independent thought-to-text claim. Different words
and montage require testing a frozen method, not transferring our five-word
model weights. Visualization can expose a competing cue/task explanation; it
does not substitute for randomized cue mappings or passive no-attempt trials.

Existing generated-only source selection work already covers all ten people,
the first common complete raw session, peripheral-preserving companions and a
10 GiB ceiling. Reuse it; do not repeat the qualification or build another
framework. Tracked records contain no real source manifest or completed
acquisition/evaluation. The old metadata-only packet remains unactivated and
does not grant payload or target access.

## Smallest next evidence target

Before any newly authorized outcomes, freeze one four-word comparison of
peripheral-only versus auxiliary-independent EEG-plus-peripheral prediction,
with matched EEG misalignment and label-shuffle controls, cue/timing controls,
and pronounced/visualization comparisons. Preserve participants and conditions;
set the exact temporal split and statistical decision after target-free source
and timing qualification, but before opening scientific outcomes. Calibrators
must be learned only within training data, equally for every learned control.

The outcome sought is incremental imagined-word information across people
beyond these recorded alternatives. A positive result would motivate a new-day,
changed-cue confirmation; a negative result would redirect the method or task.
Neither outcome alone identifies a cortical source. Do not use a language
model to turn weak closed-set evidence into apparently fluent thought decoding.

## Other reviewed sources and the actual dependency

JapanEEG **ds007808 v1.0.0** has longitudinal recordings and peripheral sensors,
but its paper's covert events occur only in sub-01; it is not a three-person
covert replication. Covert-day coverage, overlap with the used Japanese source
and a bounded slice remain unverified. [Paper, Table 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.01264v1).

SilentSpeech-EEG/BrainStack lacks a verified immutable dataset release and data
license in this refresh; its availability statement still describes preparation
and anonymization. Code licensing is not data-access permission.
[Availability statement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jacoo-Zhao/BrainStack/main/Code_and_data_availability_statement.md).

No reviewed source verified the complete fresh-day, cue-decoupled, group covert
speech and synchronized peripheral-control design. That stronger question needs
a separately scoped acquisition or collaboration. For ds003626 the immediate
dependency is explicit bounded new-source access, not another literature loop.
No participant signals, event rows, labels, private predictions or weights were
read during this source refresh; no data were downloaded or uploaded.
