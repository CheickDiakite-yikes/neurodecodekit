# Research Autonomy Charter Tier C Public Confirmation Amendment

Date: 2026-08-31

Amendment ID: `TIER-C-PUBLIC-CONFIRMATION-v1`

Status: **standing delegation becomes effective only after the separate
activation decision, this amendment, and their machine records are committed
to GitHub `main` and both required CI jobs are green**

This is an additive amendment to the approved 2026-07-15 Research Autonomy
Charter. The original charter snapshot and activation decision remain
byte-identical historical records. This amendment replaces recurring human
authorization events only inside the bounded scientific profile below. It does
not erase scientific stop rules, legal or ethical duties, machine-safety caps,
or the binding terms of a narrower registered experiment except for the one
explicit GitHub CI-read partition override stated below.

## Purpose

The original Tier C rule prevented irreversible scientific actions from being
taken by implication. It also created repeated human stops after an experiment
had already frozen every scientifically material choice. Those repeated stops
do not add protection when a standing delegation can mechanically require the
same source, target, resource, one-shot, and claim boundaries.

This amendment creates one standing profile:

`public_confirmation_v1`

After activation, a work order admitted to this profile can move from its first
permitted public-source contact through one aggregate evidence closeout without
another maintainer message. The delegation is prospective human authorization
for the profile. It is not permission to improvise.

`public_confirmation_v1` is not an open-ended self-admission mechanism. It
admits exactly the already-green `FMSR1-E2E-v0` work order bound below and no
other work order. A replication, new source work order, or later experiment may
not self-admit even if it appears to preserve the same claim class. It requires
a new additive profile revision and activation. Within the exact adopted
`FMSR1-E2E-v0` route, however, there is no later human micro-gate.

## Operations Authorized by the Standing Profile

One admitted work order at a time may perform its preregistered sequence of:

- credential-free contact with exact official public source endpoints;
- deterministic target-free discovery, eligibility testing, and selection;
- bounded metadata, manifest, license, header, and transport admission;
- bounded download of deidentified public neural and peripheral-physiology data;
- target-firewalled signal processing and development-only fitting;
- durable freezing of ordered confirmation predictions;
- exactly one delivery of the unopened confirmation target to an isolated blind
  scorer and at most one score;
- an aggregate internal evidence record under the frozen claim ceiling; and
- cleanup only of temporary files created by that invocation inside its new
  work root.

Implementation, generated-fixture qualification, coherent commits, pushes,
remote CI verification, source contact, acquisition, preprocessing, fitting,
prediction freezing, scoring, and aggregate closeout are one continuous
machine-governed route. They do not create later human micro-gates.

## Machine Admission Requirements

Admission is staged because source identity cannot honestly be known before
bounded discovery and measurement quality cannot honestly be known before
development-only processing.

### Gate A: before the first network request

The work order must bind `standing_tier_c_profile:
public_confirmation_v1`, this amendment and decision, the exact
`motor_central_increment_v1` contract, source predicate, deterministic
selection functions, code, configuration, tests, workflow, resource caps,
claim ceiling, and a new durable attempt identity. Those artifacts must be on
clean GitHub `main`, and Base Python plus Optional Neuro Readers must be green
for the exact commit. A durable exclusive lock must prove that no other
standing-profile work order is active. Every consumed source, target, marker,
and attempt identity is excluded.

### Gate B: after bounded discovery and before bulk acquisition

The selected source must be public, deidentified, credential-free, immutable
or exactly versioned, and supported by explicit license and consent terms
compatible with noncommercial research. Every required participant, member,
channel role, event role, size, source-issued cryptographic identity, geometry,
event grammar, reader, transport rule, and candidate-specific peak-storage plan
must be complete. Candidate-specific values may be resolved only by the frozen
deterministic target-free functions. Ambiguity, drift, judgment, target
dependence, an unknown request class, or cap insufficiency stops before bulk
acquisition.

### Gate C: after development processing and before confirmation delivery

All measurement and positive controls, leave-one-development-participant-out
estimates, exact final-N joint power, common-mask checks, comparator identity,
capacity matching, model freeze, prediction freeze, firewall capability tests,
and resource preconditions must pass in their frozen order. Confirmation
targets, outcomes, class-balance results, completeness results, and any other
confirmation-derived pass/fail bit remain unavailable until the single scorer
operation.

Failure at any gate consumes the attempt according to the narrower contract
and yields one measured blocker. It cannot be converted into a new source,
relaxed criterion, larger model, wider authority, retry, or smaller follow-on
authorization request.

## Scientific Integrity Invariants

The standing delegation cannot weaken these rules:

- Confirmation labels and participant outcomes cannot influence fitting,
  calibration, channel or feature selection, row or participant exclusion,
  power, thresholds, seeds, model choice, protocol choice, or source choice.
- Every development estimate used for power or sensitivity is participant-
  cross-fitted; every transform, mask, scaler, and model excludes the scored
  development participant.
- All scientifically material choices are frozen before confirmation delivery.
- Prediction workers receive signal-only derivatives and an exact allowlisted
  target-free ledger. Only the target broker may decode raw event semantics,
  and only the isolated scorer may receive confirmation targets.
- The durable prediction freeze precedes exactly one target delivery and at
  most one score. Zero post-target updates are allowed.
- `N` is the byte-identical combination of EOG, every relevant-effector EMG,
  and non-target-identical cue, timing, ordinal, and allowed metadata features.
  Posterior EEG is a distinct spatial control and is never folded into `N`.
- The correct-window central arm must beat `N` alone, geometry-matched posterior
  EEG, central pre-cue EEG, central cue-window EEG, every frozen
  structure-preserving shift, and every frozen target-blind derangement under
  the registered conjunctive intersection-union rule. No edge may be dropped,
  averaged away, selected after outcome inspection, or rescued by a larger
  model.
- Every neural arm uses the same ordered rows, byte-identical `N`, equal neural
  width, equal window duration, equal preprocessing and feature family, equal
  missingness representation, and the same estimator and fitting schedule.
  One target-blind complete-case mask is formed from all unshifted blocks before
  control mapping. Imputation, arm-specific deletion, and post-target row or
  participant dropping are forbidden.
- Joint prospective power across every edge must be at least `0.80` at the exact
  final confirmation participant count under the frozen design alternative.
  Margins come only from leave-one-development-participant-out predictions with
  every transform, mask, scaler, and model excluding the scored person.
  Participant admission floors do not imply power, and no smaller-count
  substitution is allowed. Underpowering stops before target delivery and every
  edge interpretation.
- A failed confirmation is evidence. It cannot be repaired on the same target.
- One successful cohort supports only a cohort-specific,
  participant-generalizing predictive increment under the exact recorded
  comparators. It cannot establish exclusive neural source, motor cortex,
  intention, thought, language, causal or live operation, hardware value,
  clinical value, general-population validity, novelty, or replication.
- A replication must use a disjoint, independently selected source or
  recruitment wave sealed before the preceding confirmation target is opened,
  an unchanged question, estimand, endpoint, comparator decision rule, and a
  new sealed target vault with adequate power. A source chosen after any prior
  outcome is discovery-only and cannot confirm the same estimand.

### Exact target-firewall floor

The target-free public ledger is deny-by-default and may contain only random,
source-independent and target-independent opaque row identifiers plus the exact
frozen timing and allowlisted non-target metadata fields. It may not expose raw
participant, session, run, trial, event-role, trial-type, effector, class,
label, target, target-identical metadata, or any undeclared field. Continuous
derivatives contain no annotations.

Development labels are passed only through an unlinked preopened no-follow
descriptor. The unlinked confirmation vault keeps its bare SHA private; its
only public commitment is keyed HMAC-SHA256 with an unpredictable key retained
in broker memory until delivery. Default-deny process profiles prohibit raw
member, marker, annotation, events, target-vault path, and network access by
prediction workers. Generated capability tests must prove those opens fail.

The broker emits no confirmation value, balance, cue, stratum, class-floor,
validity, completeness, or other confirmation-dependent bit before the durable
prediction freeze and target delivery. Descriptive checks occur only inside the
scorer after the score-domain check and may lower the claim ceiling only; they
cannot change inclusion, predictions, score, metric, rows, or participants.
Target deliveries are zero before durable freeze, exactly one afterward, and
score events are at most one.

## Transport, Resource, and Computer-Safety Envelope

The standing profile permits only the exact adopted work order, whose envelope
is no wider than:

- one CPU thread, one worker, and one numerical job;
- 24 hours wall time;
- 4 GiB peak process-tree RSS;
- at most three remote-green verification events: activation, implementation,
  and the hash-only prediction freeze;
- exactly three direct GitHub read requests per attempted verification event
  and at most nine across the route;
- at most 125 discovery requests and 32 MiB discovery response bytes;
- at most 256 candidate metadata, manifest, license, and header requests, at
  most 64 MiB total response bytes, at most four raw-format header reads, and at
  most 1 MiB total header bytes;

- zero retries;
- 12 GiB selected payload;
- 2 GiB invocation temporary files;
- 2 GiB derivatives and frozen predictions;
- 1 GiB atomic aggregate publication;
- 3 GiB untouched reserve;
- exactly 20 GiB maximum incremental disk across those five categories; and
- at least 20 GiB filesystem free after every allocation.

Direct TLS, stage-specific host allowlists, redirect-hop revalidation,
global-unicast peers, and zero proxies, cookies, or credentials are mandatory
for discovery, metadata, header, license, and payload endpoints. Git control
traffic is a separate class. It is limited to the exact existing
`CheickDiakite-yikes/neurodecodekit` repository and `main` branch, permits the
host's existing GitHub authentication without reading, printing, copying,
exporting, or changing any credential, and allows at most two post-activation

two pushes together at most 128 MiB; total Git response bodies are capped at 16
MiB. Tags, releases, branch deletion, history rewrite, force push, credential
mutation, and any other remote mutation are forbidden.

Each of the activation, implementation, and prediction-freeze checkpoints may
use exactly one three-request read-only GitHub verifier: current `main` ref,
exact-HEAD check runs, and the frozen workflow blob. A checkpoint not reached
uses zero requests. At most three checkpoints and nine read requests may occur,
with at most 8 MiB response bytes per checkpoint and 24 MiB total. A failed or
ambiguous checkpoint is terminal; there is no retry. These GitHub control-plane
caps are distinct from public scientific-source caps.

Every network request and response-body class must have a finite registered cap

Existing data, ignored markers, private paths, consumed roots, unrelated
worktrees, other projects, and user changes may not be deleted, overwritten,

## Durable Single-Use Rule

Before the first authorized network request, official-source contact,
raw-member open, or target operation, whichever comes first, the work order
must durably arm its exact attempt identity with no-follow, device/inode, file
and directory fsync, and created-ancestry checks. Every terminal route consumes
the attempt.

After arming or any held-out access, the work order cannot be retried, rerun,
resumed, repaired, reseeded, refrozen, substituted, or reused for model,
threshold, source, protocol, or claim selection. A transport or measurement
blocker is not a biological null. A failed scientific edge is not permission to
dataset-hop.

The global scientific lineage is outcome-blind. After any confirmation outcome,
no new work order testing the same estimand may use a source or recruitment wave
chosen after that outcome. Only a disjoint replication identity sealed before
the prior target opened remains confirmatory; all adaptive successor work is
discovery-only until a later independently sealed confirmation exists.

## Authority That Remains Human-Gated

This amendment grants no standing authority for:

- private, protected, restricted, credentialed, reidentifiable, consent-
  ambiguous, or license-ambiguous data;
- opening ignored or private evidence or any consumed target or attempt;
- human-subject recruitment, consent collection, interaction, intervention, or
  recording;
- hardware on a person, live streams, shadow/live display, or device operation;
- paid access, meaningful external cost, outreach, or communication with an
  external party on the maintainer's behalf;
- destructive Git or data operations, cross-project changes, or cleanup outside
  the invocation root;
- merge, tag, release, publication, payload upload, or a public representation
  that a scientific claim is validated;
- clinical-facing language, ethical or legal reinterpretation, or a claim above
  the exact registered ceiling; or
- any resource increase, unknown field, ambiguous predicate, or unregistered
  operation.

Those actions still require fresh, explicit human approval. Platform-level
safety restrictions continue to apply.
