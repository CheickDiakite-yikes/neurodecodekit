# Loop 48 Train-Only Hypothesis Portfolio

Date: 2026-07-15

Status: **Design research only; not preregistered, implemented, or authorized**

Machine boundary: `registries/loop48_hypothesis_portfolio.v0.json`

## Why The Hypothesis Must Improve

`F5` describes what the public Loop 26 artifacts look like: seed-sensitive
emissions and a blank-dominant primary checkpoint. It does not explain why.
Treating `F5` as the answer would collapse several plausible mechanisms into
one story and invite architecture tuning against a consumed validation result.

The stronger next design is a **parallel hypothesis portfolio**. Parallel here
means that one shared train-only evidence collection evaluates several
mechanisms at once. It does not mean running workloads concurrently. Physical
execution should remain sequential, one-worker, and one-threaded.

Stage A remains useful only as a cheap integrity and arithmetic replay. Any
Stage B below requires its own exact preregistration and authorization.

## Five Coexisting Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Distinguishing evidence | Evidence against it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `H1` | CTC feasibility or optimization failure | infeasible frame/target ratios, loss plateau, high blank posterior, weak nonblank margin, unstable gradients or updates across fixed seeds | feasible lengths, stable loss/gradient trajectories, low seed dispersion, and successful train-only sequence fitting |
| `H2` | Sensor or trial quality is insufficient | flat/noisy channels, extreme robust amplitude, line-noise burden, low repeat reliability, or bad-trial concentration | stable channel/trial summaries and reproducible task-locked signal on a train-only check split |
| `H3` | Timing or preprocessing removes/misplaces useful information | large alignment residuals, strong offline/causal representation divergence, or a train-only time-shift peak away from declared event time | small residuals, paired transform agreement, and a centered train-only timing profile |
| `H4` | The representation is stable but not separable at this sample size | stable optimization and usable signal summaries, yet simple and sequence models both fail a disjoint train-only check partition and corruption controls | a simple aligned key-window or linear probe succeeds while the sequence model fails |
| `H5` | The sentence task is prior-dominated under the available evidence | stable sensor-dependent train-only fits remain worse than a size-matched train-only no-signal prior | strict train-only gains over prior, exact-zero, label-deranged, channel-deranged, and time-displaced controls |

These hypotheses are not mutually exclusive. For example, weak signal can
amplify CTC instability, and temporal mismatch can make an otherwise adequate
representation look nonseparable. Stage B should return a support vector with
evidence completeness and conflicts, not force one winner.

## One Shared Evidence Collection

The efficient design collects each expensive quantity once and reuses it
across hypotheses.

### Static train-only audit

- per-item input length, normalized target length, and minimum CTC alignment
  requirement;
- frame-to-target ratio distribution;
- per-channel robust amplitude, variance, flatness, PSD, and line-noise burden;
- per-trial amplitude and missingness summaries;
- event-timing residuals and declared alignment uncertainty; and
- exact source, split, item, configuration, and transform hashes.

This stage can evaluate `H1`, `H2`, and part of `H3` before any parameter
update.

### Shared fit telemetry

For every future preregistered fit, log:

- loss by fixed step;
- gradient and parameter-update norms;
- blank posterior, posterior entropy, and nonblank logit margin;
- train-only CER and exact sequence accuracy;
- elapsed time, RSS, and generated bytes; and
- checkpoint/configuration identity without plaintext predictions.

The telemetry distinguishes “cannot fit the train partition” from “fits train
but does not generalize to a disjoint train-only diagnostic check partition.”

### Shared controls

Use the same train-only check rows and frozen predictions for:

- no-signal prior;
- exact-zero signal;
- label derangement;
- row derangement;
- channel derangement;
- nonwrapping time displacement;
- a simple aligned key-window or linear probe; and
- paired offline-versus-causal representations if an exact future source path
  can produce both without opening validation evidence.

One prediction bundle should answer multiple hypotheses. Do not retrain a new
model for every interpretation.

## Better Decision Logic

Stage B should report one row per hypothesis with:

- support level;
- evidence for;
- evidence against;
- missing evidence;
- conflicting evidence;
- measurements shared with other hypotheses;
- whether a causal statement is permitted; and
- the smallest next falsifying measurement.

Useful discriminators include:

1. **Cannot fit train plus blank dominance:** supports `H1`; signal quality is
   still independently assessed.
2. **Fits train, simple probe succeeds, sequence model fails check rows:**
   supports a sequence-objective or capacity mismatch more than `H2`.
3. **Simple probe and sequence model both fail while quality fails:** supports
   `H2`; do not escalate the model.
4. **Offline probe succeeds but paired causal probe fails:** supports `H3` and
   blocks a real-time claim.
5. **Stable intact-signal check performance beats corruption controls but not
   the prior:** supports `H5` without establishing useful decoding.
6. **Several rows support different hypotheses:** preserve the multi-cause
   result; do not force one primary root cause.

## Split And Leakage Firewall

The future design must create a deterministic nested partition entirely inside
the 55 source-train rows, grouped by sentence identity. Exact fit/check counts,
seeds, thresholds, and model inventory must be frozen before any train-array
read. No validation targets are permitted. The six consumed validation rows,
five source-test rows, session 2, S7, S20, and S25 may not contribute data,
labels, thresholds, selection, stopping,
or scoring.

The train-only check partition is a diagnostic partition, not a new final test.
Its result may guide whether to stop, redesign on a fresh development person,
or prepare a new preregistration. It may not rescue the consumed Loop 26 gate.

## Compute Policy

- evaluate hypotheses in parallel from shared evidence;
- execute numerical work sequentially;
- one CPU thread and one worker;
- cache shared summaries once;
- freeze one prediction bundle before interpreting any check labels;
- no restart or favorable-seed selection; and
- park on an undeclared read, cap breach, or target-conditioned change.

Exact runtime, RSS, output, fit, and model caps remain unavailable until Stage
A closes and a dependency-light static prototype measures the diagnostic
overhead without protected input.

## Primary-Source Grounding

- CTC can converge to peaky dominant-label solutions, and input-to-target
  length ratio is a relevant diagnostic: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14849
- Brain2Qwerty v1 first establishes task-locked hand/character information with
  simple time-resolved classifiers before full sentence decoding, and separates
  Conv+Trans results from language-model correction:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02303-2
- Large non-invasive language-decoding work finds strong effects of modality,
  task, training data, and test-time averaging, supporting explicit data-scale
  and protocol hypotheses:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-65499-0
- A systematic EEG study shows that preprocessing choices can materially shape
  decoding performance, supporting paired transform evidence rather than a
  preprocessing assumption:
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08464-3
- Reproducible M/EEG practice includes explicit quality assessment, temporal
  decoding, and permutation-based controls:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30127712/

These sources justify the diagnostic axes. They do not establish which axis
caused the NeuroDecodeKit result.

## Authorization Boundary

This portfolio authorizes nothing. No partition, fixture, analyzer extension,
train-array read, cache read, signal/target read, model run, training run,
prediction, checkpoint, score, download, stream, device, or hardware operation
exists. Stage B cannot inherit a future Stage A authorization.

## Claim Boundary

Engineering capability proposed: one shared train-only telemetry bundle can
evaluate several competing and coexisting failure hypotheses without reusing
the consumed validation targets.

Scientific claim not established: no hypothesis has been causally confirmed,
and there is still no neural advantage, sensor-signal dependence, decoding,
generalization, real-time, EEG, portable/home-device, or clinical result.
