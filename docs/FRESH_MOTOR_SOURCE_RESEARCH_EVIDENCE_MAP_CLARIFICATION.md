## Why This Clarification Exists

The frozen `FMSR1-v1` registry lists `posterior_EEG` inside its joint nuisance
components. That grouping collapses a neural spatial comparator into a
physiological nuisance arm. The frozen registry also does not list a
structure-preserving shifted-EEG condition among its required future
scientific conditions.

Those two details conflict with the governing attribution architecture. The
scientific distinctions below are therefore explicit for future design while
the historical `FMSR1-v1` bytes remain intact.

## Governing Scientific Architecture

The architecture is a 3D attribution cube nested inside a 5D scientific
evidence map. Translation is a separate sixth dimension.

Dimensions 1 through 3 ask **what produced the signal**:

1. **Spatial:** central EEG versus geometry-matched posterior/visual EEG.
2. **Temporal:** the correct registered motor window versus pre-cue EEG,
   cue-window EEG, and fixed structure-preserving shifted central EEG.
3. **Physiological:** real central EEG versus recorded joint physiological
   nuisance and a matched deranged-central-EEG counterfactual.

Dimensions 4 and 5 ask **what the result means and how far it generalizes**:

4. **Task identifiability and autonomy:** externally instructed protocol
   labels versus genuinely self-chosen actions.
5. **Population generalization:** same-person evidence versus untouched people
   versus an independently recruited or independent-source replication cohort.

Dimension 6 asks **whether it works prospectively in time**, and remains
separate from the evidence map:

```text
offline evaluation -> causal replay -> shadow stream -> prospective live
```

Progress on translation cannot repair failed attribution, task autonomy, or
population generalization. Live motor success also cannot establish language
decoding; communication remains a separately preregistered program with an
independently scored LLM-only baseline.

## Separable Comparator Roles

The future source-specific cube must keep these roles distinct.

### Spatial comparator

`posterior_EEG` is a separate neural spatial comparator. It is not part of the
physiological nuisance-only arm. It must be geometry-matched and evaluated in
the same registered motor window as central EEG.

### Temporal comparators

`pre_cue_EEG`, `cue_window_EEG`, and
`structure_preserving_shifted_central_EEG` are separate temporal comparators.
A shifted condition must preserve the preregistered participant, run,
cue/timing stratum, feature shape, spectral structure, missingness, and
nuisance magnitude while breaking the registered motor-time alignment.

### Physiological nuisance-only arm

The physiological nuisance-only arm is exactly the joint combination of:

- EOG;
- task-relevant EMG for every relevant effector; and
- non-neural metadata, including any preregistered cue and timing covariates.

Posterior EEG and shifted EEG are excluded from this arm because they answer
different attribution questions.

### Physiological counterfactual arm

The physiological counterfactual arm is the same joint nuisance-only arm plus
matched, target-blind, structure-preserving deranged central EEG. It must use
the same source-only calibration, capacity, preprocessing, and
participant-first scoring as the central condition.

## Every-Edge Success Rule

The candidate central condition is joint nuisance plus central EEG in the
correct registered motor window. Every neural comparator retains that identical
joint nuisance bundle and swaps only the registered EEG location, window, or
counterfactual. This capacity matching prevents nuisance information from
creating an apparent central-EEG increment. The candidate must beat every
preregistered cube edge:

1. joint physiological nuisance only;
2. joint physiological nuisance plus matched deranged central EEG;
3. joint physiological nuisance plus geometry-matched posterior EEG in the
   correct motor window;
4. joint physiological nuisance plus central EEG in the pre-cue window;
5. joint physiological nuisance plus central EEG in the cue window; and
6. joint physiological nuisance plus every preregistered
   structure-preserving shifted central-EEG condition.

This is an intersection-union requirement. All registered edges must point in
the predicted direction and satisfy their frozen minimum-effect and inference
rules. A strong edge cannot average away, compensate for, or rescue a failed
edge. Beating a no-signal prior alone is insufficient.
